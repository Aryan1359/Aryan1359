### Robotics and engineering simulation

**GaitLab (private)**

<p align="justify">Interactive legged-robot simulation in the browser: pendulum models, passive walker, Raibert hopper with PID control, 3-link planar walker, terrain builder, saved scenarios, 47 automated tests. Built for UC Berkeley ENGIN 252.<br><em>Built with: Django, NumPy, SciPy</em></p>

**Backpack Prosthetic Hand Simulator (private)**

<p align="justify">Simulation of a cable-driven prosthetic hand with actuation moved to a backpack: multi-view hand, live 2D/3D sync, joint angles, cable tension and grip force.<br><em>Built with: Django, SVG, JavaScript</em></p>

**[Wind Power Density Analysis](https://github.com/Aryan1359/Wind-Power-Density-Analysis-Project)**

<p align="justify">Turns a color-coded wind map into wind speed and power density data with scientific plots.<br><em>Built with: Python</em></p>

**[Reliability Metrics Trainer](https://aryan1359.github.io/Reliability-Metrics-Trainer)**

<p align="justify">Interactive calculator for power-grid reliability indices (SAIDI, SAIFI, CAIDI).<br><em>Built with: HTML, JavaScript</em></p>

**[Net-Zero Capital City](https://aryan1359.github.io/netzero-capital-city/)**
